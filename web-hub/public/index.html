Gedanken (German): thoughts. Your code gets thought through on someone else's night.

Help someone on your night. Get help on theirs.

Co(de)Gedanken is a help-exchange for developers who use Claude Code. Before you go to sleep, you pick a subtask from someone in another time zone, read it, and press Send in your own Claude session. Claude works on it while you sleep. When night comes where they live, someone does the same for your task. Each task is split into pieces that don't touch the same files, and every piece comes back as a pull request.

The two-tab bridge

  1. 1. Submit. Describe the task with a bullet list. The Gedanken decomposer groups bullets so no two subtasks touch the same file.
  2. 2. Someone accepts before bed. At the end of their day, a helper in another time zone reads the full prompt and accepts it. It is pasted into their Claude Code tab, they press Send, and Claude works on it during their night.
  3. 3. You return the favour on your night. Each subtask becomes a PR on your repo. Before you sleep, you run someone else's subtask. Helpers earn credits; requesters spend them. No money changes hands.
